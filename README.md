# Astral Nexus RPG 1.28.0 — Paper 1.21.4

Astral Nexus is an optional fantasy-RPG dungeon adventure for Paper 1.21.4 and Java 21. The dungeon pool still contains four maps; the first three are redesigned as long, narrow, oppressive cave systems. The fourth, **Костяная бездна**, keeps its separate bone-and-sculk identity.

## Three claustrophobic cave tunnels

The visual direction is inspired by the practical cave-set design of *The Descent*: cramped passages that constrict into low throats, short pockets that briefly open before the next narrow stretch, rough natural rock, sharp dripstone and almost total darkness. The first three maps remain about 157 blocks long, but their usual walkable width is now about 9–13 blocks rather than a wide cavern hall. The ceiling follows the sloping floor and dips lower at selected choke points.

1. **Сумрачные галереи** — a forgotten deepslate mine. The tunnel is lined with broken supports, cracked masonry, gravel-like rubble and tiny gilded seams buried in the rock. Light is scarce and recessed; the passage repeatedly tightens between old braces.
2. **Аметистовый разлом** — a narrow calcite-and-basalt fissure. Small amethyst veins are embedded into the walls rather than growing into the player's path. Uneven black rock, close stalactites and occasional low pockets make it feel like a geode that is closing in.
3. **Изумрудные недра** — a root-choked underground passage. Moss, rooted dirt and hanging roots cling to the margins; short dripstone teeth and collapsed fragments break up the floor edges while the centre remains traversable.
4. **Костяная бездна** — the separate fossil cave with bone ribs, basalt ridges, sculk growths and its own larger architectural language. Its overall geometry is intentionally kept distinct from the three narrow routes.

## Natural shapes, darkness and decoration

- The first three maps use narrower corridors, irregular low ceilings and deliberately pinched sections. The floor still descends and rises smoothly, with solid rock underneath every ramp and landing.
- Two-block rock boundaries follow the tunnel and close off the sides. Rough ledges, fallen fragments, ribs and dripstone are attached to the margins instead of blocking the central walking line.
- Side grottos on the first three maps are small pockets, not enormous extra halls. The final boss room is also reduced to a compact chamber with a clear entrance and enough room for the encounter.
- Ambient lighting is intentionally sparse. Small soul-lit niches are far apart; there are no regular rows of bright lamps or glowing floor strips. The fourth map retains its own lighting and formations.
- The route remains dry. Mob spawns still use the actual local floor height, and the three original encounter checkpoints and boss remain connected to the sloped route.

## Random map selection

The player cannot receive the same cave twice in a row. Selection remains random among the other available routes, and a route is reserved while its owner is inside so it is never regenerated under another player.
